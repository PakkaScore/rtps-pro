# Privacy Policy — RTPS Pro

RTPS Pro is 100% on-device. No server, no account, no analytics.

**What we store:**
- Profiles you save (name, address, caste, etc) in `chrome.storage.local` on your device only
- Drafts auto-saved while typing (7-day expiry)
- Settings (language, theme)
- History metadata only (profile name, form type, timestamp) — no field values

**What we never store or send:**
- Photos, captchas, OTPs, passwords, file inputs
- No network requests of our own
- No analytics, no trackers

**Permissions:**
- `storage` — save profiles locally
- `activeTab`, `scripting` — read/fill form on serviceonline.bihar.gov.in when you click Auto-fill
- `contextMenus`, `tabs` — right-click menu, open RTPS home
- Host: `https://serviceonline.bihar.gov.in/*` only

Uninstalling removes all data.
